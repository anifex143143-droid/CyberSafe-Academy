🛡️ What You Learned

Social-engineering scams often use urgency, fear, authority, and unexpected requests to pressure people into making unsafe decisions.

  • Never share an OTP with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.
  • Do not click suspicious or unexpected links.
  • Knowing some personal information does not prove someone's identity.
  • End suspicious calls instead of continuing the conversation.
  • Verify messages through the organization's official app, website, or phone number.
