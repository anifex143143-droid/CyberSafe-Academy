What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disruption.

Common Cyber Threats

  • Phishing
  • Malware
  • Password attacks
  • Social engineering
  • Unsafe websites and downloads

Basic Safety

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Be careful with unexpected links and attachments.
  • Use multi-factor authentication when available.
